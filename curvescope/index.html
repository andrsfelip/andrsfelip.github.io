01 / OVERVIEW

The project

An applied fixed income project in C++20, focused on U.S. Treasury valuation, interest-rate risk, and yield-curve positions.

Objective

Evaluate relative value and translate a view on the yield curve into a position whose exposures can be measured and explained.

Scope

Bond pricing, duration, convexity, and DV01 form the analytical foundation. Steepener and flattener positions connect these measures to curve analysis and scenario assessment.

Analytical focus

Distinguish a position’s intended exposure from its residual risks. Assess hedge ratios and rate scenarios alongside the assumptions behind each calculation.

02 / BASICS

The foundations

Price & yield

A bond’s value reflects its future cash flows and the rates used to discount them. For fixed cash flows, a higher yield implies a lower price.

Duration & DV01

Duration describes first-order sensitivity to yield changes. DV01 expresses the approximate dollar price change for a one-basis-point move.

Convexity

The price–yield relationship is curved. Convexity captures the second-order effect that a linear duration estimate leaves out.

Curve positions

Steepeners and flatteners express views on yield differences across maturities. Matching DV01 reduces first-order parallel-shift exposure while leaving other risks.
